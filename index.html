9 places to explore responsibly

From Everest to the Terai, every trip uses small groups, local guides and community-run stays. Prices are per person in Nepali rupees (NPR) and include stay, meals, permits and guide.

Levels: Easy means short walks at low altitude. Moderate means 4–6 hours of walking a day. Challenging means high altitude and long days, so good fitness is needed.

Our promise

Every booking follows four rules, checked each season with the communities we work with.

Local income

At least 70% of your price stays in the village: homestay families, porters, guides and farmers.

Low waste

Carry a refillable bottle. We fund water refill points so no plastic bottles are used on the trail.

Nature first

Fixed group sizes, marked trails, and a per-trip contribution to forest and wildlife rangers.

Culture respected

Guests join festivals and farm work by invitation, with a briefing on local customs before each trip.
